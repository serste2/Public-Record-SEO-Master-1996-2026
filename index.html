Position

A practice built through images, systems and places

Serena Stelitano has spent much of her working life moving images out of the places in which they were expected to remain. Drawings became editorial commentary and conversations. Conversations entered programmable systems. Digital works acquired printable and physical counterparts. Exhibitions became buildings; buildings became reporting environments; data became something that could be walked through or worn; investigations eventually became games.

Her formal training combined drawing and composition with restoration and cataloguing. Later professional work added editorial illustration, digital collaboration, blockchain-native art, international virtual curation, regenerative farming, journalism training and XR production. These practices developed at different times and for different purposes. Across them, Stelitano repeatedly works with provenance, classification, access, spatial organisation and the consequences of participation.

Since 2021 she has worked with the Center for Collaborative Investigative Journalism on immersive investigations and public experiences. Her current public profile also records a parallel role as regenerative farmer and manager in Tuscany and her direction of bottega di Serste, founded in 2014.

01

1996–2018

Drawing Before the Interface

The earliest part of Stelitano’s practice belongs to drawing: editorial illustration, comics, natural-history subjects, cultural heritage and visual responses to public issues. Pitoti / PITOON translated the rock engravings of Valle Camonica into contemporary narrative form. The Black Cat approached Poe through sequence, atmosphere and character. Political and social images were developed alongside sports illustration and visual journalism.

Carugate e il Parco degli Aironi (2014) came from a local mobilisation around the proposed expansion of the Carosello shopping centre. Emanuele Lecchi developed the original concept and reference images; Stelitano produced the drawings. Roads, traffic, trees and development pressure become the visual vocabulary of a territorial conflict.

Ucki: Diary of a Stone-age Boy combined archaeological interpretation, character design and educational publishing. Its Pleistocene fauna memory game existed as a digital printable designed to become physical cards. The screen already functioned as one stage in a longer route toward handling and play.

The 2016 portrait Andrea Camilleri, cassatina sulla terrazza a Marinella, produced during the SeLoPhlee period, later developed an independent editorial life under an open licence. Its continued circulation across newspapers, cultural magazines, journals and Wikiquote made a single illustration part of Camilleri’s public visual record.

02

2015–2020

Drawing With Others

DADA organised drawing as visual conversation. An image could answer another image, alter the direction of a sequence and remain legible as an individual contribution within a shared exchange. Stelitano participated through visual conversations, live drawing, early cryptoart, charity activity and later experiments around generative systems and token economies.

Creeps & Weirdos placed work by the DADA community inside an early blockchain-native collection concerned with scarcity, provenance, editions and continuing relationships among artists. Stelitano’s participation belongs to the period before NFT culture developed its later commercial conventions.

Soul in the Machine / DADAGAN extended collaborative drawing into a live hybrid system involving human drawing, generative processing and physical/digital presentation. The work belongs to a period in which Stelitano increasingly treated the image as one component inside a larger event.

03

2019–2022

Programmable Images, Physical Consequences

With TXU.TOTEM, collaboration entered the technical structure of a programmable artwork. The five-person TXU collective contributed states to a master whose visible composition could change. Serste’s states included SNK, HEN, FRL and SOL. The work survives as Async Art master token 108 and as a record of collective programmable authorship.

Oracle, programmable fortune-teller distributed control among the artist, the owners of seven Sibyl leaf layers, an autonomous Apollo component and the person consulting the work. The leaves could be changed between positive and negative states, altering the current reading. Stelitano also developed explanatory writing, participatory events, derivatives and a printable version composed of background art, cards and a booklet.

Andrea Grows carried interaction into publishing. The webcomic used branching choices and connected DADA drawing processes, NEAR/Mintbase publishing, NFT distribution, QR access and a physical collector box. The reader moved between web interface, printed material, object and post-purchase experience.

Across Ucki, Oracle and Andrea Grows, the digital file repeatedly acquires another state: printable, collectible, physical, playable or shared.
04

2020–2023

Building Places for Art

Stelitano’s first years in Cryptovoxels/Voxels combined curation and building. PYF — Presente y Futuro of the Human Civilization brought Global South artists into a navigable virtual exhibition. Solo exhibitions, shared venues and collective projects followed. DADA HOUSE translated a drawing community into a place assembled from images, objects and rooms.

Rare Effect Vol. 1 joined a physical cultural programme in Lisbon to a virtual gallery modelled after Arroz Estúdios. Wildeverse connected a German festival to spaces in Cryptovoxels and Somnium. In Naples, a digital twin of the catacombs became a setting for cryptoart. These environments developed a spatial vocabulary before investigative reporting became their principal content.

05

2021–2023

When Reporting Becomes a Place

With CCIJ, Stelitano began applying spatial design to investigative reporting. Her documented workflow follows the reportage from development, researches locations and background further, adapts visual materials, maintains adherence to data, shares test-builds with the team, refines them technically and editorially, tests public access conditions and preserves versions as digital history.

An Encroaching Desert Intensifies Nigeria’s Farmer-Herder Crisis translated reporting on environmental pressure and conflict into a voxel environment. Rivers of Sewage expanded the format into welcome rooms, reconstructed spaces, learning areas and data rooms. Not the Kind of Life a Human Being Should Live, Direct Potable Reuse and the Russian oil investigation developed further relations among photography, reconstructed environments, external geographic tools and visitor movement.

06

2022–2024

Walking and Wearing Data

Stelitano’s data rooms increasingly gave datasets scale and position. Walk the Data organised wastewater compliance information spatially. In Direct Potable Reuse, the virtual environment opened outward through a Google Earth connection, allowing the room to act as one point in a larger geographic reading.

Dreadlocks and Discrimination moved data onto the visitor’s avatar. Categories of reported discriminatory experience were translated into three-dimensional wearable objects. Harassment, refusal, targeting, being stopped, being beaten, arrest and related responses could be selected and worn. The data visualisation entered the social and bodily behaviour of the gathering.

07

2023–2025

Three Degrees of Participation

The XR layer developed around Democracy Deferred was organised across three levels of interaction. In Voxels, visitors explored photography, reporting, video, audio, voxelised magazines and reconstructed obstacles associated with election malfeasance. In Zappar, Shield Your Vote added place-and-play AR: polling-place objects entered the user’s surroundings, while a ballot-container lid became a shield against malfeasance smoke and attacking social posts. Fortnite then gave the player an investigative role and a persistent game environment.

Shield Your Vote accumulated 360° scenes, a playable polling-place sequence, data visualisation and Wear the Data. The project treats access, information and action as parts of the same mobile experience.

08

2025–2026

The Investigation Becomes a Game

The Data Vault Canyon moved the investigative system into Fortnite UEFN. The environment is organised around a damaged newsroom, recovered Data Fragments, fog, verification and a final decision about the fate of information. Design work covers level structure, visual metaphor, UI, NPC systems, progression, data recovery and platform compliance.

The project’s design dossier assigns investigative functions to roles such as editor, on-site journalist, data scientist and AI-chatbot. Fog represents engineered confusion and information manipulation; Data Fragments represent material that must be recovered and checked. Later level concepts extend the system toward coordinated bot activity in The Bots Ridge.

The current Data Vault level ends with two actions: Release the Data and Seal the Data. The player reaches that screen after spending the level locating and protecting the material at stake.

09

2019–2026

Ground, Climate, Writing

Since 2019 Stelitano has also worked as a regenerative farmer and manager in Tuscany. Land management, biodiversity, climate and public policy increasingly enter her writing and XR work. Her 2025–2026 writing includes long-form journalism on professional trauma among investigative journalists, environmental and historical analysis of the Maremma, and work around agriculture, zoonotic risk and regenerative futures.

Forest Is Life — But Nigeria’s Last Rainforest Is Falling Fast connects reporting to an XR data layer in which temperature differences are translated into placeable AR elements. Thermal conditions become part of the reader’s immediate environment. Puddle Placer AR extends an earlier line of work in which data gains dimension, position and behaviour.

Selected provenance & circulation

Works that continue outside their first context

TXU.TOTEM #108. Async Art master; five-person collective programmable artwork. Auction and authorship records survive, with Serste among the contributors.

Oracle. Async programmable master and layers, with related printable and MakersPlace derivatives. Its surviving public documentation records layer logic, collector agency and participatory use.

Andrea Grows. NEAR/Mintbase publishing combined with an interactive webcomic, collector box, QR flow and physical distribution.

Andrea Camilleri portrait. The 2016 open-license portrait has circulated through at least nine independently accessible editorial/periodical contexts plus multiple Wikiquote uses. The documented circulation includes La Voce di New York, Biblioteche oggi, Treccani, Il Sole 24 Ore/Nòva100, CulturaIdentità, 4U Magazine and Incontri.
